# Discord Permissions

## Overview

PyPSA-meets-Earth is a collaborative open source initiative dedicated to building a transparent, inclusive, and reproducible energy system modeling ecosystem, leveraging the PyPSA framework and contributing to the broader scientific and policy discourse around sustainable transitions. This document outlines the rules of and permissions to our communication channels.

## Server Rules

All members of the Discord server follow the [Code of Conduct](https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/). In addition, the following rules apply:

- Keep discussions related to open energy system modelling and the PyPSA ecosystem. Use `off-topic` for anything else.
- No advertisement or promotion of services or products. Job, funding, and event opportunities related to the energy transition go to `jobs-funding-events`.
- Be respectful and constructive. Harassment and spam are not tolerated.
- Use the channel that fits your topic: ask for help in `support`, share your work in `show-off`, and prefer threads for longer discussions.
- Ask questions in public channels where possible, so that others can benefit from the answers.

Moderators may remove content that violates these rules. Repeated or severe violations may lead to removal from the server.

## Channel Structure

| Category | Purpose | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | General chat, `support`, `show-off`, `off-topic`, `jobs-funding-events`, voice channels | Everyone can read and write |
| Contributors | Existing developments in the PyPSA ecosystem | Everyone can read; Contributors can write |
| GitHub updates | Development updates from GitHub | Read-only |
| Project channels | Communication of projects applying PyPSA ecosystem tools, on request to a Coordinator or the Steering Committee | Visible (encouraged) or private, as requested |
| Coordination and maintenance | Coordinator, moderator‑only, and maintenance channels | Restricted, see below |

## Roles and Permissions

The table below defines how the governance roles of PyPSA-meets-Earth map to Discord roles and which permissions are associated with them.
It is intended as a practical reference for configuring and maintaining the Discord server while ensuring transparency, minimal privilege, and role‑aligned decision structures.
Communication is kept as open as possible and as closed as necessary: private channels are used only where they serve a clear purpose, such as maintenance or coordination.
Project‑ or stream‑specific channels may require additional case‑by‑case permission settings to ensure relevant contributors have access.
Maintenance channels remain restricted to designated individuals, independent of governance roles.

| Governance Role | Discord Role | Permissions Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant | @everyone | Read and write in the Community category; read access elsewhere; no moderation rights. |
| Contributor | Contributor | Write access to the Contributors category; self‑assigned by reacting in the rules channel; project‑ or stream‑specific access may be granted case‑by‑case. |
| Coordinator | Stream Coordinator | Moderation rights within own stream; access to coordinator channels (if applicable); may create project channels; extended permissions in project‑ or stream‑specific channels possible. |
| Steering Committee | Steering Committee | Server‑wide moderation rights; access to the moderator‑only channel; may manage channels where needed; access to maintenance channels. |
| – | Server Admin | Technical administration only (server settings, bots, integrations, role assignment as agreed). Moderation is carried out under the holder's governance role, not the admin role. |

Access to the moderator‑only and maintenance channels is limited to the Steering Committee and to individuals explicitly granted access.
Members may additionally self‑assign a time‑zone role (Americas, Africa/Europe, Asia/Oceania) to ease scheduling.

## Transition

The current server uses the roles `@Core` (active developers with maintainer rights) and `@Working Group` (active developers, assigned on merit), and grants access to the moderator‑only channel to a wider group than the table above. Existing access is not withdrawn abruptly:

- `@Core` maps to Stream Coordinator, or to Steering Committee for its members.
- `@Working Group` is kept until it is mapped to a governance role.
- Changes are announced in advance and discussed with the affected members.
